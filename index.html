Past and future.

The filter uses only observations available at each time. The smoother makes a second, backward pass using later observations. Its narrower uncertainty is retrospective, not a forecasting advantage.

Dynamics and covariance

State is [position, velocity], with F = [[1, Δt], [0, 1]]. Continuous white acceleration has Q = q[[Δt³/3, Δt²/2], [Δt²/2, Δt]]. Position is observed with variance R. The prior applies at the first observation time.

Accepted readings use a Joseph-form covariance update. Missing and gated readings skip measurement correction. Rauch–Tung–Striebel smoothing works backward over the accepted measurement set. Matrices are checked for finite, positive-definite covariance.

What the bands mean

Bands show marginal mean ± 1.96 model standard deviations. They rely on the supplied linear Gaussian model. Gating selects observations based on their values, so these bands do not account for the selection event and are not guaranteed 95% coverage after outlier rejection.

NIS is squared innovation divided by predicted measurement variance, computed before gating. Its displayed average includes rejected readings and excludes missing ones. RMSE and empirical position-band coverage use only supplied reference truth; those fields are unavailable without truth.

Bounds and reproducibility

2–500 observations, timestamps 0–1,000,000, successive gaps 0.1–10. Source JSON is limited to 200 KB. Processing is deterministic and local. Exports include every prediction, update, covariance, innovation, smoothing gain, and result. This is a one-dimensional motion model, not a tracking system for live hardware.
